Week 1 — Ciphers, Fundamentals & Hashing 12 topics · 15 questions →
Classical ciphers (Caesar, Vigenère, Bifid, Rail Fence), Shannon's perfect secrecy, One-Time Pad, symmetric vs asymmetric intro, hashing (SHA, MD5), salting, HMAC, data states, Kerckhoffs' principle, computational security
Week 2 — Asymmetric Crypto & Key Exchange 10 topics · 15 questions →
Diffie-Hellman key exchange, RSA algorithm, public/private key pairs, trap-door functions, digital signatures, certificates, PKI, key management, hybrid encryption, man-in-the-middle attacks
Week 3 — Secure Design & Usability 12 topics · 15 questions →
"Why Johnny Can't Encrypt," "Why Cryptosystems Fail" (Anderson), "The U in Crypto," IEEE Top 10 Secure Design Flaws, OWASP cryptographic failures, cognitive walkthrough, barn door property, abstraction property
Week 4 — Zero Knowledge Proofs 10 topics · 15 questions →
ZKP properties (completeness, soundness, zero-knowledge), interactive vs non-interactive proofs, Ali Baba cave analogy, zk-SNARKs vs zk-STARKs, Fiat-Shamir heuristic, applications in blockchain and privacy
Week 5 — Zero Trust Architecture 10 topics · 15 questions →
NIST SP 800-207, zero trust tenets, PE/PA/PEP architecture, trust algorithm, micro-segmentation, software-defined perimeters, deployment models, identity-centric security, "never trust, always verify"
Week 6 — AES & Algorithm Selection BONUS · Not on Midterm →
AES competition history, Rijndael selection, block cipher modes (ECB, CBC, CTR, GCM), DES to AES evolution, algorithm evaluation criteria, symmetric key sizes, S-boxes, substitution-permutation networks, Feistel ciphers
